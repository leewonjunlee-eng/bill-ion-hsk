THE HSP QA PATH · IMPLEMENTED, TESTED WITH STUBBED GATEWAY REPLIES

How a payment is guarded end to end

1Three signatures
All wallets sign the same revision, amount, parties, token, chain and expiry.
2One order per room
A stale or unpublished revision cannot reserve.
3Match, then sign
The EIP-712 / x402 request must equal the approved terms.
4No blind retry
A lost reply never triggers a second submission.
5Verify on chain
HSP status, both order IDs, Transfer and AuthorizationUsed logs.

These gates cover payment authority issued through Bill:ION. They do not claim the HSP Gateway enforces revisions.
